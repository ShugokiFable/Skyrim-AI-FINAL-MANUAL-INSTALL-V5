HEADROOM + GROK (MCP only - do NOT wrap Grok inference)
----------------------------------------------------
Headroom compresses context. It can also proxy a CLI's inference traffic,
BUT its Grok proxy talks to https://api.x.ai and needs XAI_API_KEY.

If you sign in to Grok with a SUBSCRIPTION (the normal login), your endpoint
is https://cli-chat-proxy.grok.com - Headroom cannot forward that. Wrapping it
breaks Grok: the model shows as "unknown", grok-4.5 disappears, and every turn
fails with 401. v5.0 of this pack applied that wrap automatically. v5.2 does not.

The installer now only registers Headroom as an MCP server, which gives Grok
headroom_compress / headroom_retrieve / headroom_stats. That is on-demand
compression, not automatic traffic compression. For a subscription account it
is the only mode that works.

    .\TOOLS\Ensure-Headroom-Grok.ps1             MCP registration (auth aware)
    .\TOOLS\Ensure-Headroom-Grok.ps1 -CheckOnly  report only
    .\TOOLS\Ensure-Headroom-Grok.ps1 -Repair     undo a v5.0 wrap that broke Grok
    .\TOOLS\Ensure-Headroom-Grok.ps1 -Wrap       opt in (refuses without XAI_API_KEY)

GROK SHOWS "unknown" MODEL? Run:

    .\TOOLS\Ensure-Headroom-Grok.ps1 -Repair

then open a NEW PowerShell window and run: grok
